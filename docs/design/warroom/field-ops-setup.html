What is a doctrine?

The rulebook a team plays by. It says how the team sequences its response, what it must never do, when it hands over to another team, and what good looks like. It is used in three places:
  • Building the scenario — injects and expectations follow it.
  • Judging players — the AI judge scores decisions against it.
  • AI-operated teams — they act from it.
